01 — Context

Overview

Bloopy is a mobile app designed to centralize pet care services — walking, grooming, and veterinary visits. Users can manage multiple pets, track their full service history, and communicate with caregivers, all in one place.

The Problem

  • Pet services scattered across multiple disconnected platforms
  • No unified service history per individual pet
  • Low user retention in service-based apps

The Goal

Design a user-friendly platform that simplifies pet care management and increases long-term retention through a reward-based virtual currency system — Snowflakes.

02 — Process

04 — Engagement System

Snowflakes

One of Bloopy's key differentiators is a gamified virtual currency system designed to drive long-term retention and reward loyal users for consistent platform engagement.

❄️
Virtual Currency System
Users earn Snowflakes for every booking, review, and referral. They can redeem them for discounts, free services, or exclusive features — creating a reward loop that makes the app compelling beyond the immediate transaction.
05 — Final UI Design

Visual System

The final UI balances warmth and playfulness with the clarity needed for a service-booking experience — ensuring pet owners feel confident and engaged throughout.
