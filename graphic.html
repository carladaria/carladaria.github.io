Bloopy Cat Food

Happy cats, happy life. BLOOPY is a conceptual brand of natural food and snacks for cats, blending nutrition, wellbeing and a fun, friendly, contemporary visual identity. The goal: a recognizable, coherent brand able to stand out in the pet market through an attractive, emotional visual language. The creative direction is playful, minimalist and modern, with pastel colors, rounded typefaces, cat illustrations full of personality and dynamic compositions. The campaign covers packaging for food, snacks and supplements, advertising product photography, social media pieces and merchandising — building a warm, memorable and versatile universe that connects emotionally with cat lovers.

Scorpio Fuego

Handle the heat. Brand presentation for Scorpio Fuego, a spicy food line spanning hot sauces, tortillas, chips and snacks, and guacamole. The identity channels the sting of the scorpion and the fire of Mexican chili: a bold, high-contrast visual language with a charged red-and-black palette, sharp expressive lettering and a scorpion emblem with attitude. The system covers logo, color and typography, a packaging family with a heat-level scale, product photography direction and social media pieces — a daring brand that turns every meal into a dare.
