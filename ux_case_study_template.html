01 — Context

Overview

Cinematic is a centralized platform that unifies every movie theater in the country — regardless of brand — into a single ecosystem. Users browse films, compare theaters, purchase tickets, and order candy from one unified app or website.

The Problem

  • Theater brands operate in isolated, incompatible ecosystems
  • Users forced to navigate multiple apps per chain
  • Inconsistent UX across competing cinema platforms
  • No centralized ticket + concession purchasing flow

The Goal

Design a unified cinema platform that simplifies movie discovery, streamlines ticket purchasing, and integrates candy ordering — while remaining profitable through a minimal 2% service fee per transaction.

06 — Business Model

Value

Business Model

Cinematic operates on a minimal 2% service fee per transaction — making it highly attractive for theater partners while sustaining platform growth.

  • No exclusivity requirements for cinema partners
  • Lower fees than existing ticketing incumbents
  • Additional revenue through featured placements

Value Proposition

A single platform for users. A low-cost distribution channel for cinemas. A scalable, centralized system that serves the entire movie-going ecosystem — from discovery through post-purchase.
